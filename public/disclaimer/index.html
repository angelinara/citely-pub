What the pages contain

Each topic attempts to make reference to official sources, with added links. Alongside the sources is a plain-English reading of the substance. The readings and any summaries are ours, and they can be wrong or incomplete.

Where the law applies

The topics refer to the law of England and Wales. They may not apply in Scotland or Northern Ireland, and in some areas the law in Wales differs from England.
